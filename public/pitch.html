The problem

Kids learn space facts.
Astronauts live by trade-offs.

Most space learning tools are quizzes and fact cards. Real missions are about hard choices: every watt you give the radiation shield is a watt your greenhouse doesn't get.

  • Who it's for: students aged 8–14, classrooms, and anyone curious about Artemis and Moon-to-Mars.
  • What they should walk away with: systems thinking. Power, air, food and radiation are all connected, and a choice in one place shows up somewhere else.
  • Why a game: you remember the day your crew nearly ran out of oxygen far better than a paragraph about life support.

  • Each morning you see the forecast and split the power between four sliders.
  • Run the day. The live 3D habitat, crew health and a plain-language day report show what your choice did.
  • Choose a world, a difficulty, and a class code so a whole class gets the same space weather.
03 / 07

User experience

Real space weather hits your base.

  • Incidents from real science: solar particle events, Martian dust storms, Jupiter's radiation belts. Each card names the NASA instrument that measured it.
  • 15-second interventions: hands-on mini-games like aligning the magnetic shield or resetting breakers.
  • Live data: a space weather badge pulls today's flares from NASA DONKI and the Kp index from NOAA SWPC.
04 / 07

Learning, not just playing

Every mission ends in a debrief.

  • "Why did this happen?" A root-cause view traces a failure back to the decision that caused it.
  • Teacher tools: class codes, a printable STEM lab report, and a certificate for each student.
  • For every learner: 6 languages, a colour-blind-safe palette, reduced motion, works offline as an installable app on iPads and Chromebooks.
05 / 07

NASA data that makes it possible

The numbers come from real missions.

  • LRO Diviner & LOLA — lunar temperatures and sunlight at Shackleton crater
  • LRO CRaTER — lunar radiation dose
  • LCROSS — water ice in shadowed craters
  • Curiosity RAD & REMS — Mars radiation and weather
  • Mars 2020 MOXIE — making oxygen from Mars air
  • Opportunity — the 2018 global dust storm
  • Europa Clipper — Jupiter radiation and ice
  • Cassini-Huygens & Dragonfly — Titan's atmosphere and lakes
  • Kilopower / KRUSTY, MMRTG — space power systems
  • NASA-STD-3001, ISS ECLSS — crew air, food and dose limits
  • NASA DONKI + NOAA SWPC — live space weather feed
  • Apollo 13 records — the "Cold Cabin" scenario
